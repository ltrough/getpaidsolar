APS has launched a Storage Rewards pilot program that allows qualifying homeowners with battery systems to earn $110 per kW compensation for supporting Arizona's electrical grid during periods of high demand.

💰 APS Will Pay You For Extra Solar Credits

If your solar system generates more energy credits than you use, APS lets those credits accumulate throughout the year. At your annual reconciliation, if your remaining credit balance is more than $25, APS will mail you a check for the remaining balance.
